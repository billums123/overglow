How it works

Pixels above the threshold are mapped from SDR reference white (203 nits) up to your chosen peak, converted to BT.2020 primaries, PQ-encoded (SMPTE 2084) with Floyd–Steinberg dithering, and written as a JPEG with an embedded Rec.2100 PQ ICC profile generated from macOS ColorSync.

Good to know

  • The glow only shows on HDR displays, with low-power mode off
  • Screenshots and most re-saves strip the profile
  • Flat graphics work best — photo gradients can band at 8-bit
  • Re-processing a file that has already been through overglow goes mushy
